How I work.

Four steps with every client, whether it's a website, a video or a page to grow.

  1. 01

    Connect before
    anything else.

    We talk through what you need, who it's for and when you need it. No guessing.

    Client

    I need Reels for my page.

    Me

    Got it. Who's watching, and when do you need them?

  2. 02

    Plan it
    on paper first.

    I map out the pages, the script or the content before I build anything, so we agree on the shape early.

    The plan

    • Goal and audience
    • Script or page outline
    • Assets and deadline
  3. 03

    Build it,
    and keep you posted.

    I design, code, edit or post, and send updates along the way instead of disappearing until the deadline.

    In progress

  4. 04

    Revise together
    until it's right.

    You review it, I make the changes, and we repeat until you're happy to put your name on it.
